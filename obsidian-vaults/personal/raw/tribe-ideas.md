# Tribe Ideas

## 2026.02.14.

1. Profile verification (like on Tinder), to calm down users, that the one who they match with is not a bot/catfish etc.
2. Upload photos (selfies, fancy meals etc). taken from the connection/activity. This is a) verification that the connection took place, and b) can use later for creating memories for user (adds to retention!)
3. @User Story: "Optionally continue chatting with the AI to enrich or refine their profile" <- let's restrict the number of prompts, so they won't burn many tokens.
