# Sole Proprietorship (EV)

[[wiki/entities/molnar-benedek]] operates (or operated) as an egyéni vállalkozó (EV — sole proprietor) in Hungary. Extensive bookkeeping records on OneDrive under `Könyvelés - EV/`.

## Records

- **Flat-rate tax calculations** (Átalányadó kalkuláció): Annual templates from 2022 through 2026
- **Tax documents**: SZJA (personal income tax) for 2022, hIPA (local business tax)
- **Balance statements**: Dr. Molnár Benedek EV balance confirmations (2025.12.31)
- **Startup documents**: `Indulás/` folder
- **Contracts**: `Szerződések/` folder
- **Invoices**: `Számlák/` folder
- **Closure**: `Zárás/` folder, `tevekenyseg-megszuntetesek.pdf` (activity cessation)
- **Tax balance**: `adoszlaegyenleg_20220701.pdf`

## Tax Method

Uses the **átalányadó** (flat-rate taxation) method, which is a simplified tax regime for Hungarian sole proprietors.

> [!note] Status: Active
> The EV is confirmed active as of 2026-04-19. The `Zárás/` (closure) folder and `tevekenyseg-megszuntetesek.pdf` may relate to a previous activity code being wound down while the EV itself continues under a different classification.
