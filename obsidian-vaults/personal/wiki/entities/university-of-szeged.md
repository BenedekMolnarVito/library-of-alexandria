# University of Szeged

The primary alma mater of [[wiki/entities/molnar-benedek]], located in Szeged, Hungary. Three distinct programs completed here across a decade (2010–2022).

## Programs

### Biology BSc and MSc (2010–2015)

Foundation in biological sciences. Degree certificates available in both English and Hungarian.

### English-Hungarian Scientific Translator (Postgraduate, 2013–2015)

Specialized postgraduate program for scientific translation between English and Hungarian. This is a rare skill combination that informs the bilingual nature of this Personal Vault.

### Neuroscience PhD Program — Doctoral School of Biology (2015–2022)

Advanced research in [[wiki/concepts/neuroscience]]. Conference presentations at FENS, HunDoc, IBRO, and SZAB. Research involved thalamus-related neuroscience (evidenced by Edward G. Jones thalamus PDF on OneDrive).

## Academic Output

- Scientific conference posters and presentations (FENS, HunDoc, IBRO, SZAB)
- MATLAB work for electrophysiology analysis (matlab4ephys.pdf on OneDrive)
- PhD-related documents on OneDrive (phd_sz_2018_2.doc)
