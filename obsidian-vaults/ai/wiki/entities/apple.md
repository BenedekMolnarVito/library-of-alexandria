# Apple

Apple matters in this wiki less as an AI lab than as the hardware platform making local AI increasingly viable. Apple Silicon shows up repeatedly as the physical substrate for running larger models privately, cheaply, and with enough performance to make local-first workflows practical.

## Role in AI Landscape

Within this corpus, Apple is part of the infrastructure story: memory bandwidth, efficient local inference, and consumer-hardware clustering are making local AI more competitive with cloud-only assumptions.
