# Gergely Orosz

Gergely Orosz is the author of *The Pragmatic Engineer*, consistently rated as the top-ranked technology newsletter on Substack and one of the most influential publications for working software engineers. He is known for in-depth, interview-driven reporting on engineering organizations, career development, and — increasingly — the impact of AI on software development practice.

## Background / History

Orosz was a software engineer at Skype, Microsoft, and Uber before transitioning to full-time writing and content creation. His engineering background lends credibility to his reporting — he understands the technical details of what he covers and can probe interview subjects with the depth that a pure journalist often cannot. The Pragmatic Engineer has grown to hundreds of thousands of subscribers and is frequently cited as required reading for senior engineers and engineering managers.

## Key Contributions / Features

**Deep-Dive Interviews**: Orosz conducts unusually thorough interviews with engineers and leaders at frontier AI companies, extracting implementation details and design decisions that are rarely publicly documented. His interview with [[wiki/entities/boris-cherny]] on building [[wiki/entities/claude-code]] is one of the most detailed accounts of how a major AI coding tool was conceived and architected. (Source: [[wiki/sources/building-claude-code-boris-cherny]])

**Steve Yegge Interview**: His conversation with [[wiki/entities/steve-yegge]] on the death of IDEs provided a structured forum for one of the most provocative theses in the AI coding space. (Source: [[wiki/sources/from-ides-to-ai-agents-steve-yegge]])

**Uber Agentic Engineering Case Study**: Orosz documented Uber's internal shift to agentic engineering workflows — including the striking statistic that some teams moved from 20 engineers to 2 while maintaining throughput — providing one of the most concrete enterprise case studies in the corpus. (Source: [[wiki/sources/uber-agentic-engineering-shift]])

**Product-Minded Engineers in the AI Era**: Covered the evolving role of product-minded engineers as AI changes what "building software" means, addressing how engineers who understand both product and engineering can extract outsized value from AI tools. (Source: [[wiki/sources/product-minded-engineers-ai-native]])

## Role in AI Landscape

Orosz's role is as the most credible journalist in the "AI for engineers" space — combining technical depth with the journalistic discipline to get primary sources on record. The interviews he publishes often become the canonical reference for understanding how specific tools and organizations actually work, rather than how they are marketed. His coverage of Claude Code, agent teams, and enterprise AI adoption is some of the most cited material in engineering discussions.
