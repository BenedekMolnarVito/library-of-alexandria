# MiniMax

MiniMax is an AI lab in the open-model ecosystem that shows up in this corpus through two related themes: aggressive price/performance competition and early public discussion of self-evolving agent scaffolds. The newer M2.7 materials position MiniMax as one of the clearest examples of open-model vendors pushing beyond raw benchmark talk into harness-level optimization and agentic workflows.

## Key Characteristics

- Competes on price/performance rather than pure frontier prestige
- Publicly discusses harness self-evolution and agentic evaluation loops
- Offers developer-friendly access paths through token plans and Anthropic-compatible APIs

## Role in AI Landscape

MiniMax matters here as evidence that self-improving agent patterns are not limited to the biggest Western labs. It also reinforces the corpus-wide theme that open-model competition is compressing cost quickly while forcing labs to show real agentic utility instead of only benchmark marketing.
