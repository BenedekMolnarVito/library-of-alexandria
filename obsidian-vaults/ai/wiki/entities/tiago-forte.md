# Tiago Forte

Tiago Forte is the best-known popularizer of the "second brain" idea in contemporary personal knowledge management. In this corpus he mainly appears as conceptual lineage: the LLM wiki inherits the externalized-knowledge ambition of the second-brain movement, but automates the maintenance burden that made human-kept systems expensive to sustain.

## Role in AI Landscape

Forte is relevant here as a predecessor figure. The corpus repeatedly contrasts older PKM methods with LLM-maintained markdown systems, making his work a useful anchor for what is being extended or replaced.
