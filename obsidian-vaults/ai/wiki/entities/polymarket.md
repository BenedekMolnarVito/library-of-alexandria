# Polymarket

Polymarket is the prediction-market platform used in this corpus as a clean demonstration of intelligence arbitrage. Because its contracts, pricing, and lag windows are visible and measurable, it becomes an unusually transparent arena for showing how AI compresses reasoning and execution gaps.

## Role in AI Landscape

Polymarket functions as a laboratory for AI-enabled arbitrage: a place where the difference between "AI attached to an old process" and "process rebuilt around AI" can be measured in real profit and in shrinking time windows.
