# Milla Jovovich

Milla Jovovich is a public co-creator and principal face of [[wiki/entities/mempalace]], an open-source AI memory project that became highly visible in 2026. Across sources, her role is consistently framed as conceptual and architectural leadership around long-term context retention.

## Role in AI Landscape

Her involvement materially increased attention to AI memory architecture and helped move "agent memory" discussions into mainstream developer channels. The MemPalace launch also became a case study in how visibility, benchmarking, and open-source scrutiny interact.
