# Mo Gawdat

Mo Gawdat is a former Chief Business Officer at Google X and a prominent AI macro commentator focused on social, economic, and ethical consequences of rapid AI deployment. In this corpus he appears less as a builder of specific systems and more as a strategist warning that labor markets, education, and entrepreneurship are entering a high-volatility transition period.

## Key Characteristics

- Frames the next decade as a difficult transition before a possible AI-enabled abundance
- Emphasizes agility, ethics, and skepticism as core survival skills
- Treats AI as an intelligence amplifier that should augment thought, not replace it

## Role in AI Landscape

Gawdat adds a macro lens missing from many builder-first AI sources. Where practitioners focus on harnesses, skills, and infrastructure, he focuses on labor displacement, power concentration, and the social operating system needed to survive mass capability shocks.
