# Amazon

Amazon appears in this corpus as one of the clearest case studies of large-scale AI-assisted engineering under real production pressure. Its importance here is not model development but organizational learning: layoffs, internal coding acceleration, and the resulting dark-code failure modes forced Amazon to move toward more explicit spec-driven development and stronger comprehension discipline.

## Role in AI Landscape

Amazon is the "what happens when this meets a giant production system" example. The company illustrates that AI coding leverage at scale is not just a productivity story; it is also a governance, review, and system-understanding story.
