# I make fun websites on the side and it pays my rent, literally

**Authors**: Gigi Creates
**Date**: 2026-04
**Type**: article

## Summary

Gigi Creates (Jenn) describes website flipping as a passive side hustle: building no-code WordPress blogs around personal interests, growing them briefly, and selling on digital marketplaces for $500–$1,000+ each. The author made ~$30K in 2025 doing this without cold outreach, marketing, or coding — relying entirely on marketplace demand. The article is mostly promotional for the author's course 'The Art of the Microflip.' This source is weakly connected to the AI/tech batch — the article mentions 'vibe coding' as an alternative to no-code builders — situating it in the broader 2026 AI-accelerated indie developer and creator economy.

## Key Takeaways

- Website flipping: build, briefly grow, sell no-code WordPress blogs on digital marketplaces
- Small sites sell for up to $500; established sites start at $1,000+
- No client acquisition, no marketing needed — marketplaces bring buyers
- Made ~$30K/year as a side hustle building blogs about personal interests (travel, coffee, matcha, bubble tea)
- Beginner-friendly: no coding required, builds take a few days to weeks
- Author now runs a course on the model
