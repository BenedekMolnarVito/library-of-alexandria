# Anthropic And OpenAI Are Fighting Over Your Memory. You're Going To Lose.

**Authors**: Nate B Jones
**Date**: 2026-04
**Type**: video-transcript

## Summary

Nate B Jones argues that professionals are inadvertently building their most valuable career asset — AI context — inside platforms that are deliberately designed to trap it. He defines four layers of professional AI context (domain encoding, workflow calibration, behavioral relationship, artifact history), explains why each layer is sticky and hard to port, and frames the lack of portability as a genuine market failure. Neither the model providers nor the emerging memory startups have solved it — providers lack the incentive, and the pain point is too diffuse for a candy product to gain traction.

## Key Takeaways

- The 'honing effect': AI systems adapt to your cognitive and behavioral patterns through hundreds of micro-corrections — making them feel like a personalized intelligence rather than a generic tool.
- Four layers of professional AI context: (1) Domain encoding — industry vocabulary, competitive landscape, internal acronyms; (2) Workflow calibration — how you structure research, preferred code review formats, memo templates; (3) Behavioral relationship — unstated preferences, when to challenge vs execute, how technical to go; (4) Artifact history — demonstrated capability context from past work.
- 60%+ of workers use personal AI for work regardless of IT policy — the context problem is real and largely unaddressed.
- Platform incentives: none of the model makers want to solve portability — they all want to keep you inside. Every platform makes it easy to get context in and hard to get context out.
- Memory startups are failing to capture people where they feel the pain — it's a diffuse pain point, not an acute one (candy product vs opium product in product strategy terms).
- 90% of professionals will face this problem in the next two years through job changes, AI tool changes, or company policy shifts.
- Solution direction: treat professional AI context as a career asset to be nurtured and made portable — needs a BYOC (bring your own context) standard for enterprise workers.
- Major companies including Meta are flying candidates in and locking them in a room with their tools to test AI capability — because there's no credential system for AI skill.
