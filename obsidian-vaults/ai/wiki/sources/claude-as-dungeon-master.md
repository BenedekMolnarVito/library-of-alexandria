# Claude as a Dungeon Master: Let's Check It Out

**Authors**: HShorter - It's Gaming
**Date**: 2026-04
**Type**: video-transcript

## Summary

A gaming YouTuber evaluates Claude as a Dungeons & Dragons Dungeon Master, comparing it to prior experiments with ChatGPT and Gemini. Claude was asked to build an HTML D&D app with choice menus, dice rolling, HP/gold tracking, and immersive narrative. The author notes Claude produces significantly more detailed and literary prose than competing models, and successfully incorporated manual dice rolls after real-time code modification. Overall assessment is strongly favorable, prompting a paid subscription.

## Key Takeaways

- Claude generated a fully functional HTML-based D&D app with stat tracking, choice menus, and dice rolling from a single prompt.
- Claude's narrative writing quality was noticeably superior to ChatGPT and Gemini for RPG storytelling.
- The app was modifiable in real-time during the session (e.g., adding manual dice rolls on request).
- Claude successfully handled combat mechanics, NPC dialogue, and atmospheric worldbuilding.
- Free tier has message limits (ran out mid-session); author decided to subscribe.
- Claude's strength in creative/literary writing is a consistent trait noted across many contexts.
