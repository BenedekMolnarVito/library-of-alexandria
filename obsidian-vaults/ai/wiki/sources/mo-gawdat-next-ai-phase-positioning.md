# Ex-Google Exec: How to Position Yourself Now Before the Next AI Phase (2026–2027) | Mo Gawdat

**Authors**: Mo Gawdat interviewed by Silicon Valley Girl
**Date**: 2026-03
**Type**: video-transcript

## Summary

Mo Gawdat offers a macro-strategic, intentionally alarming view of the near future: AI will compress product-building timelines, hollow out repetitive and middle-layer work, and force a much more agile model of entrepreneurship and career positioning. His core advice is not to deny the shift but to become excellent at using AI, stay adaptable, and keep human judgment active instead of outsourcing thought wholesale.

The source is strongest not as a forecast to accept literally, but as a framing device for the social and economic instability that may sit underneath the otherwise builder-optimistic AI narrative in this wiki.

## Key Takeaways

- Expect a major labor-market shift within the next few years, especially in repetitive and junior-heavy functions
- Entrepreneurship is becoming less like long-horizon chess and more like constant high-speed adaptation
- AI should be used to expand human intelligence, not replace thinking with passive trust
- Ethics, agility, and skepticism are treated as survival skills, not side concerns
- Education and credentialing are likely to be destabilized by direct AI-assisted capability building
