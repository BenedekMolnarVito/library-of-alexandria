# There Are Only 5 Safe Places to Build in AI Right Now. Are You in One?

**Authors**: Nate B Jones (AI News & Strategy Daily)
**Date**: 2026-04
**Type**: video-transcript

## Summary

As AI commoditizes software production — Lovable alone creates 100K+ apps daily — the middleware trap eliminates companies that are thin wrappers around foundation models. The author identifies five durable verticals that AI structurally cannot replace: Trust (verification and accountability layers), Context (proprietary data ownership and permissioning), Distribution (discovery gatekeepers), Taste (editorial judgment and orchestration quality), and Liability (accountability governance for regulated industries).

Companies surviving the middleware trap own something structural that model providers cannot replicate: runtime infrastructure (Replit), deployment infrastructure (Vercel), data gravity (Notion, Salesforce, Epic, Palantir, Snowflake), distribution monopoly (Google, Apple), or accountability (Stripe, professional services firms). The author's test: "What do I own that still matters if AI gets 10x better?" If a better model makes a product obsolete, the positioning must change now.

## Key Takeaways

- The middleware trap: vibe-coded apps with no structural moat are replicable in a week — most will die; a few will become platforms
- Surviving companies own structural assets model providers cannot replicate: Replit owns runtime, Vercel owns deployment infrastructure, Notion owns context/data gravity
- Trust: Stripe's "Powered by Stripe" is a trust signal, not a technical feature — critical when agents autonomously transact on your behalf
- Context: companies with proprietary structured data (Salesforce, Epic, Palantir, Snowflake) own the choke point — agents need context to be useful
- Distribution: when supply is infinite, curation is the scarcest resource; agent discovery is an emerging massive problem
- Taste: orchestration quality — the human who carefully tunes prompts and designs workflows creates an experience no model can auto-generate
- Liability: regulated industries (healthcare, legal, finance) sell accountability; AI makes liability governance more important, not less
- Ask: "What do I own that still matters if AI gets 10x better?"
