# AI Is Turning $21.5 Trillion Into Dust

**Authors**: Mandar Karhade
**Published**: 2026-04-12
**Type**: Long-form economic analysis

## Summary

A data-driven examination of AI's wealth transfer mechanism. U.S. labor's share of GDP hit 53.8% (lowest ever recorded since 1947). Seven companies (the Magnificent Seven) own 37% of S&P 500, up from 12% a decade ago. 56% of CEOs admit getting nothing from AI investments, yet mass layoffs continue. Piketty's r > g (return on capital exceeds economic growth) has become the default with AI as the accelerant.

## Key Takeaways

- **Massive wealth extraction** — U.S. labor share fell 6 percentage points (58% → 53.8%) in ~45 years
- **Concentrated ownership** — Top 1% owns exactly 50% of all U.S. corporate stock; top 20% owns 93%
- **Productivity paradox** — 95% of enterprise AI pilots fail to deliver ROI, yet 16,000 net jobs erased per month
- **r > g on steroids** — Capital returns 12x wage growth; AI accelerates the gap
- **Regressive energy tax** — Data center electricity drives up power bills for poor; benefits accrue to capital owners
- **Entry-level collapse** — Junior hiring declined 67%; new AI jobs require master's degrees
