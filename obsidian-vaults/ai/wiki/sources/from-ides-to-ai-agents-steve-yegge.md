# From IDEs to AI Agents with Steve Yegge

**Authors**: The Pragmatic Engineer (Gergely Orosz, interviewing Steve Yegge)
**Date**: 2026-03
**Type**: video-transcript

## Summary

Steve Yegge, veteran software engineer (Amazon, Google) and creator of Gas Town (an open-source AI agent orchestrator), discusses his 8-level framework for AI adoption among engineers, the 'vampire burnout' effect of high-productivity AI work, and his prediction that big tech companies are quietly dying as small 2–20 person teams become able to match their output. He argues that AI will cause ~50% engineering job losses at large companies while simultaneously enabling a bottom-up innovation explosion, and predicts that by end of 2026 most people will program by talking to a face/avatar interface.

## Key Takeaways

- 8 levels of AI adoption: Level 1 = no AI; Level 6 = bored waiting for agents; Level 7 = managing orchestrated agent networks.
- The 'vampire burnout' effect: AI makes you 100x more productive but only for 3 good hours a day.
- Gas Town is Steve's open-source orchestrator: one 'mayor' agent + task workers, with two worker archetypes — min-context (PCats) and max-context (Crew).
- Big tech companies are at an inflection: laying off ~50% of engineers while funding the remaining half with AI tooling.
- Token burn rate is the single best proxy metric for organizational AI adoption.
- The death of the IDE is happening — cloud code/agent UIs replace it, and Steve predicts programming-via-face-avatar by end of 2026.
- Dr. Eric Meyer (inventor of C, Visual Basic, Haskell): 'The days of coding by hand are over' — cited as major inflection point signal.
- Model half-life at Anthropic dropped from ~4 months (early 2025) to ~2 months (early 2026).
