## How much can you make website flipping?

Small sites sell for up to $500 each.

Larger, established sites sell starting at $1,000 or so.

I’ve flip sites nearly every single year, on the side. It’s fun for me to create something from scratch, grow it and then sell it as a valuable digital asset.

My buyers are:

- Business owners
- Solopreneurs
- Marketers
- Investors
- Website flippers
- Hobbyists
